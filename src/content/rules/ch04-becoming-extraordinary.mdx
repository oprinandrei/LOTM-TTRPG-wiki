## Promotion: Taking a Potion

To become a Beyonder — or advance to the next Sequence — you need a **potion** made from main ingredients and auxiliary ingredients.

### Ways to Obtain a Potion

1. **Brew it yourself:** Obtain the formula through the Beyonder community or other channels, gather all main and auxiliary ingredients, brew the main ingredients into liquid, add auxiliaries in order, and bottle it.
2. **Buy a finished potion:** More expensive than brewing and rarer, but skips the ingredient-gathering process. Often the only option when a supplier refuses to share the formula.
3. **Take the raw ingredient directly:** Simply consume the main ingredient without preparation. Extremely high risk of losing control — essentially a gamble.
4. **Organizational promotion:** Official or affiliated Beyonders may request promotion through their organization after accumulating merit. The organization will supply formula, ingredients, and assistance — but this comes with oversight, restrictions on your actions, and limited opportunities for demigod-level advancement. At the demigod stage, only finished potions are provided (no formula or process).

## The Promotion Sanity Check

The moment you take a potion, roll an immediate **Sanity Check**:

> Roll **1d20 + 20** against your **current Sanity.**
> - Result **≤ current Sanity:** Success
> - Result **> current Sanity:** Failure

*(Note: There are no critical successes or critical failures on promotion Sanity checks.)*

### Modifiers to the Promotion Check

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Each point of same-sequence Digestion | −1 (favorable) |
| Each auxiliary ingredient in the potion | −1 (favorable) |
| Taking raw main ingredient (no potion processing) | +5 (unfavorable) |
| Main ingredient is a Beyonder Characteristic with personality similar to yours | −2 (favorable) |
| Main ingredient personality is *opposite* to yours | +4 (unfavorable) |
| Currently in a state of madness | Final Sanity loss halved (round up) |
| Each undigested lower-sequence potion in your body | +1 (unfavorable) |
| Taking a duplicate potion of the same Sequence level | Penalty changes from +20 to +40 until digested |
| A Beyonder of the same or higher Sequence provides environmental support (ritual/requiem/guidance) | −2 (favorable), −1 additional per Sequence level above yours |
| Promoting under a full moon | −2 (favorable) *(does not guarantee no mutation)* |
| Promoting under a blood moon | −4 (favorable) *(risk of mutation even higher)* |
| Creature is a non-intelligent animal | +4 (unfavorable) |

## Promotion Ceremony

You may hold a **promotion ceremony** when advancing a Sequence:

- **With a ceremony:** Gain 5 points of Sanity protection (deducted from Sanity loss first).
- **Without a ceremony:** Sanity loss at the end of promotion is **doubled**.

### High-Sequence Promotions (Sequence 2+)

Followers provide Sanity protection for Beyonders at this level:
- **With ceremony:** +1 Sanity protection per 1,000 followers.
- **Without ceremony:** +1 Sanity protection per 5,000 followers.

## Promotion Outcomes

| Sanity Lost | Result |
|---|---|
| Less than 3 | **Success** — see below |
| 3 points | **Semi-out of control** |
| 5+ points | **Out of control / Death** |
| Already semi-out of control when promoting | Counts as having already lost 3 points |

### Semi-Out of Control (during promotion)

Make a **Difficulty 15 Will check:**
- **Pass:** Promoted successfully.
- **Fail:** Make an additional 1/1d2 Sanity check (roll 1d20 vs. current Sanity). If this causes total Sanity loss to reach 5+, proceed to Out of Control/Death.

All promotion modifiers still apply to this secondary check.

### Out of Control / Death

Make a **Difficulty 20 Will check:**
- **Pass:** You fail to promote but survive as a special "failed promotion" Beyonder (see Chapter 7: Special Rules).
- **Fail:** Roll 1d2 — on a 1 you go out of control; on a 2 you die.

### Successful Promotion

You become the corresponding Beyonder. However, for **72 hours** after promotion:
- Spirit Vision will reveal you as a newly promoted Beyonder.
- All checks using your new Beyonder abilities: **−2 (unfavorable).**

## Duplicate Potions

Taking multiple potions of the same Sequence level has these effects:

| Duplicates | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 extra | All Beyonder ability checks and escape difficulty checks at that Sequence level: **+2 (favorable)** |
| 2 extra | Beyonder ability strength increases by 1 Sequence level (GM determines the precise benefit when no clear upgrade path exists) |
| 3+ extra | GM determines benefits (e.g., increased ability caps, reduced negative effects) |

> The GM should moderate the power of duplicate potions carefully. They impose greater burdens on digestion than they grant power — for example, taking 10 potions of the same level should not result in automatic critical successes.

## Interchangeable Pathways (Path Jumping)

At the threshold of **Sequence 5 → Sequence 4**, most pathways can no longer continue up their own line — instead, they jump to an **adjacent pathway.** (The three Fool/Gate/Error paths jump at Sequence 4 → Sequence 3.)

### Path Jump Rules

**Retained abilities:** All Beyonder abilities from your original path are kept permanently at the level you had them. They don't upgrade further, but they still count as demigod-level effects if you're at Sequence 4 or higher — including personality suppression.

**Ability mutation:** If you hold potions from two different pathways at the same Sequence level simultaneously, those abilities may mutate into strange hybrid effects. Discuss the nature of the mutation with your GM. Generally, the number of mutated abilities should not exceed the number of distinct-pathway potions at the same level.

### Jumping Multiple Times

You may jump paths more than once (e.g., Seer → Apprentice → Thief → Seer), but each jump adds more undigested potions to your body, increasing the difficulty of future promotions.

### Forced Jumps (Non-Adjacent or Invalid)

Forcibly jumping to a non-adjacent pathway (outside the valid groups) results in **permanent semi-madness** and the **loss of any future promotion opportunity** — the next attempt will inevitably result in loss of control. (See Chapter 7 for details on the "failed promotion" state.)

### Inheriting Low-Sequence Abilities after a Jump

After jumping, you gain the attributes and skill bonuses from all lower-sequence potions of the new path. However, if your original path already raised an attribute to the same or higher level, that attribute won't improve again.

**Incomplete jumps (missing low-sequence characteristics):** If you take, for example, only a Sequence 4 potion of the new path without its Sequence 9–5 characteristics inside it, you only gain Sequence 4 abilities. You can fill in the lower abilities later by taking lower-sequence potions of that path.

A path jump is **upward-compatible only** — you cannot horizontally or downward combine pathways.

### Adjacent Pathway Groups

| Group | Pathways |
|---|---|
| The Fool's Three | Seer (Fool), Apprentice (Gate), Error (Wheel of Fortune) *(jumps at Sequence 4 → 3)* |
| Five of Omniscience | Storm, Sun, Visionary, Hanged Man, Reader (White Tower) |
| Two of Disaster | Demoness (Witch), Red Priest |
| River of Eternal Darkness Three | Night (Darkness), Death (Grim Reaper), God of War (Twilight Giants) |
| Two of the Land of Disorder | Dark Emperor, Justicar |
| Knowledge Itself Two | Hermit, Secant |
| Two of Desire | Chained (Bound), Abyss |
| Two of Life | Moon, Earth (Mother) |
| Key of Light | Destiny *(no adjacent path — stands alone)* |

> **Hidden adjacency:** The two Disaster paths (Witch, Red Priest) are secretly adjacent to the three River of Eternal Darkness paths (Night, Death, God of War). However, a character who jumps between these groups cannot reach the Old Days rank unless they first separate out their prior characteristics.

## Acting (Digestion Through Roleplay)

Taking a potion grants power — but that power doesn't truly belong to you until you **digest** it. Digestion means **acting out the role** described by your potion's name. If you truly lose yourself in that role and forget you are merely playing, you risk losing control.

The balance: *commit to the role, but never forget you are acting.*

### Digestion Progress (Digestibility)

Each potion has a **Digestibility cap of 20.** After promotion, the corresponding potion starts at 1 Digestibility.

**Digestion Bonus:** For every 5 Digestibility points, your HP max and Spirituality cap each increase by 1 (duplicate potions excluded).

**Full digestion:** When a potion is fully digested, you'll hear an illusory shattering sound and feel something dissolving and merging into your spirit. This confirms complete digestion.

### Gaining Digestibility

**Method 1 — Daily Acting**

Regularly embodying your role in daily life (a Seer running a fortune-telling practice, a Pharmacist healing patients, etc.):

| Sequence | Time per Digestibility Point |
|---|---|
| Sequence 9 | 1 week |
| Sequence 8 | 2 weeks |
| Sequence 7 | 1 month |
| Sequence 6 | 3 months |
| Sequence 5 | 6 months |
| Sequence 4 | 1 year |
| Sequence 3 | 3 years |
| Sequence 2 | 6 years |
| Sequence 1 | 15 years |

*(Sequence 0 still uses the Sequence 1 rate, but you must digest three separate Sequence 1 characteristics.)*

**Method 2 — Decisive Acting Events**

Completing a significant, role-relevant accomplishment that is recognized by others, or earning recognition from higher-sequence Beyonders:

- While doing daily acting: **+1d6 Digestibility**
- Without daily acting: **+1d4 Digestibility**

### Advance Acting (Pre-playing a Higher Role)

You may begin acting out a higher-sequence role before you've been promoted to it. After actual promotion, you inherit any pre-acted Digestibility. However:
- Pre-acting daily progress: **half speed** (double the time requirement)
- Pre-acting decisive events: **1d3** instead of 1d6, or **1d2** instead of 1d4

### Digestion Without Any Acting

| Sequence | Time per Digestibility Point |
|---|---|
| Sequence 9 | 2 months |
| Sequence 8 | 6 months |
| Sequence 7 | 10 months |
| Sequence 6 | 2 years |
| Sequence 5 | 5 years |
| Sequence 4 | 20 years |
| Sequence 3 | 40 years |
| Sequence 2 | 80 years |
| Sequence 1 | 200 years |

> Once you choose to act, the passive digestion rate no longer applies — you only use the acting rate.

## Spirit Vision

Every Beyonder possesses **Spirit Vision** — a special perception that allows them to see the spiritual layer of reality. Its exact appearance and power vary by sequence and pathway.

**Activation:** 1 free action (requires a specific physical trigger you've established — pinching your brow, clicking your teeth, etc.).

Spirit Vision allows detection of other Beyonders, extraordinary items, spirits, spiritual phenomena, and more. The full details of what each sequence can perceive depend on their pathway.

## Sanity, Madness, and Loss of Control

### Recovering Sanity

Three methods of recovery per 24-hour period (each independent):

1. **Psychological Guidance:** A Psychiatrist-path Beyonder (or similar) applies therapeutic techniques. Roll 1d20 + Psychology + relevant attribute against the patient's current Sanity. Retry permitted once per 24 hours (once per hour for Visionary path; once per 30 minutes at Sequence 7).
2. **Full Rest:** 8 hours of sufficient sleep restores **1 Sanity.** Sleep within a 12-hour window counts. Once recovered, this method cannot be used again for 24 hours.
3. **Entertainment:** At least 2 hours of genuine, personal enjoyment restores **1 Sanity.** What constitutes enjoyment depends on your character. Once per 24 hours.

> All three methods can be used within a single day, restoring up to **3 Sanity total.**

---

### Sanity Checks (General)

When subjected to a frightening, supernatural, or traumatic event:

> Roll **1d20.** If the result is **≤ current Sanity:** success (lower loss). If **> current Sanity:** failure (higher loss).

A Sanity loss is written as **X/Y** — X on success, Y on failure.

Some effects add a **penalty to the check** (e.g., "+2 unfavorable"). This means you roll 1d20+2, making it easier to fail (harder to pass).

**Critical Failure only** on the Sanity check: a natural 20 is a critical failure — you automatically take the maximum possible loss. There is no critical success.

---

### Semi-Out of Control (Madness, 3+ Sanity lost at once)

- You feel intense pain and collapse — unable to take Move actions.
- Using Beyonder abilities immediately triggers a **1/1d2 Sanity loss.**
- Roll **1d2** for duration in rounds.
- Each round on your turn: make a **Difficulty 10 Will check** or suffer **0/1 Sanity loss.**
- Your body begins to display mythic/out-of-control symptoms.
- The GM rolls a **madness tendency** (see below) that you must act on while semi-out of control.
- You may resist a madness-driven action with a **Difficulty 15 Will check.**
- When the duration ends (if resisted), you return to normal. Physical symptoms take 5 minutes of rest to fade; hair/nail growth, however, remains until cut.

---

### Uncertain Madness (Sanity below half of maximum)

When your remaining Sanity drops below half your Sanity limit (round up), you enter **Uncertain Madness.**

- Roll **1d8** every 12 hours to determine your current madness symptom.
- These symptoms are **internal** — no external signs — but all behavior must conform to the rolled tendency. The GM may apply it in a subtle way.
- Unlike semi-out of control, you can reroll if the result is situationally impossible.

**Ongoing effects of Uncertain Madness:**
- Every 24 hours: make a **0/1 Sanity check.**
- Full rest no longer restores Sanity (unless aided by extraordinary means — the Night path provides this by default).
- If Sanity falls below 3 during Uncertain Madness **or** you remain in Uncertain Madness for 1 week: a **second personality** begins to form.

**Exiting Uncertain Madness:** Restore Sanity to above the halfway threshold by any means.

---

### The Second Personality

If a second personality takes root:

1. **Early stage (no external symptoms):** Breaking Uncertain Madness before the second personality has existed 1 month prevents it from taking hold.
2. **After 1 month:** You will begin hearing its voice (inaudible to others).
3. The second personality knows everything you know, but uses your deepest fears and insecurities against you, pulling you toward loss of control.
4. The GM plays the second personality. If the second personality persists for another month, it continues even if you exit Uncertain Madness — causing all ongoing Uncertain Madness penalties.

**Clearing the Second Personality:**

- **Psychotherapy:** A Psychiatrist Sequence 7+ must treat you once per day. Base duration: **5 years.** Reduced by 1 year per Sequence level of the therapist above 7, and another 1 year per Digestion point (minimum: 1 week of daily sessions).
- **Mental combat:** Enter the deepest layer of your mind (the dream's bottom) and fight the second personality directly. It has stats equal to yours. If you die in this inner battle, you become a vegetable. Psychological guidance will work on it. Generally requires outside assistance.

---

### Full Loss of Control (Sanity reaches 0)

When Sanity hits 0 under any circumstances, you immediately and **irreversibly** lose control. Your character is effectively dead — transformed into an out-of-control creature.

**Out-of-Control by Sequence:**
- **Before Sequence 4:** The creature has no human traits — no mythical semi-form. Pure monster with enhanced attributes.
- **Sequence 4+:** The creature retains special benefits from its sequence and poses a serious threat.
- **Sequence 2+:** Loss of control can be **reversed** if someone helps you regain Sanity. But they must face a "boss"-level version of you to do it.

**Post-loss-of-control creature type:**
- Before Sequence 2: Classified as undead — takes special damage from anti-undead effects.
- The spirit thread remains but cannot be puppeted (attempting it causes spiritual pollution to the puppeteer).
- After death, leaves behind Extraordinary characteristics and/or items carrying severe spiritual pollution. These cannot be safely used and inflict harm on anyone who tries.

| Roll | Madness Tendency |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Violent:** You use irrational, forceful methods to address problems. |
| 2 | **Avoidance:** You prioritize escaping the current situation over solving it. |
| 3 | **Memory Loss:** Your thoughts scatter; you cannot rationally process the situation. |
| 4 | **Fainting:** Your vision nearly blacks out — you hang on by a thread of consciousness. |
| 5 | **Hysteria:** Suppressed emotions burst — screaming, laughing, weeping, or panic. Possible fear effect. |
| 6 | **Paranoia:** You fixate entirely on what matters most to you right now; nothing else exists until madness ends. |

| 8 | **Dependency:** You act exclusively to protect whoever is closest to you. If alone, to protect yourself. |

## Mental Solidification

If you have repeatedly suffered the same type of Sanity shock over time, weaker versions of that shock begin to affect you less.

**Condition:** You have survived at least **3 instances** of the same category of mythic shock without dying.

**Effect:** When suffering any Sanity loss weaker than that threshold (excluding promotion checks), subtract 1 from the final Sanity loss (minimum 0).

This solidification is permanent.

## Witnessing Mythological Forms

Whenever you witness a being manifesting its mythological creature form, you suffer a Sanity loss and gain knowledge.

*(Runaways before Sequence 4 do not provide knowledge — only Sequence 4+ mythological forms do.)*

### Sanity Loss by Relative Sequence

| Relative Sequence | Sanity Loss | Check Modifier |
|---|---|---|
| Low-to-mid sequence runaway | 0/1 | — |
| 1 Sequence higher than you | 1d8 / 2d8 | +4 unfavorable |
| 2 Sequences higher | 1d12 / 2d12 | +6 unfavorable |
| 3 Sequences higher | 2d12 / 3d12 | Instant loss of control/death (unprotected) |
| 4+ Sequences higher | 3d10 / 4d10 | Instant loss of control/death (unprotected) |

**Immunity:** From Sequence 4–3, witnessing an incomplete mythological form between those levels causes no Sanity loss. From Sequence 2 onward, witnessing other mythological forms causes no Sanity loss at all.

### Knowledge Gained from Witnessing

Each time you witness a mythological form, roll **3 × 1d4** for knowledge gained (no repeats — reroll if duplicate):

| Roll | Knowledge Type |
|---|---|
| 1 | Path-related information (unconcealed, not anti-divinated) |
| 2 | Potion formula for that path (1d2 copies; high-Sequence formulas require Difficulty 20 Luck check for completeness) |
| 3 | The mythological form owner's name, title, personality, or stance (roll 1d4 to choose which) |
| 4 | Ritual knowledge, ability usage techniques, or other lore (GM determines specifics) |
