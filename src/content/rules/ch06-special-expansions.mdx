## Expansion 1: Playing as a Mortal (Not Extraordinary)

Potions are not mandatory. Players may choose to play as a legendary mortal investigator — someone who survives and even triumphs in a world of Beyonders through sheer skill, preparation, and grit.

**Rules:** Mortals cannot obtain potions by any means. In exchange, they receive the following:

**1. Access to Heavy Weapons**
Mortals may carry rifles, shotguns, and even steam rifles, plus armor types not normally permitted. Discuss specifics with your GM.

**2. One Sealed Item** (choose one or discuss with GM):
- *Murloc Sleeve Spike:* Gain 5 Armor and the ability to breathe underwater. Downside: intense thirst on land — all checks −5 if you haven't drunk water in the last 10 minutes.
- *Sun Emblem:* Once per day, emit blinding light. All non-Sun-path creatures in range take 1d6 damage; undead take 4d6. Downside: if carried for 24+ hours, you become a raving sun-worshipper.
- Other Sealed Items with GM approval.

**3. A Special Background** (choose one):
- Your family enshrines a powerful spirit — it answers prayers and offers protection. Name it.
- Your family owns a mine — Reputation increases by one level.
- You are a sincere believer — the gods observe you as an anchor in their plans.

**4. Legendary Investigator Stat Bonus**
Starting Education +3; all other attributes +1 (Education still cannot exceed the human cap of 6).

**5. All-Mortal Groups**
If the entire party is playing as mortals, these rules apply to everyone.

## Expansion 2: Official Church Members

*"We are guardians — and a group of wretched people forever fighting against danger and madness."*

In Lord of the Mysteries, supernatural events occur constantly. Wild Beyonders work alone and are not trusted by society. The Orthodox Churches field special teams to contain these threats and keep the veil intact.

When at least one official Beyonder is in the party, ask your GM whether to activate this expansion. The GM may selectively enable or disable specific benefits for balance.

### Joining an Official Faction

Choose one church to join. You may have limited contacts in allied churches, but not access to their key secrets.

**Churches by Region:**

| Region | Church | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Loen Kingdom** | Church of Evernight | Dominates land-based territories |
| | Church of Storms | Dominates island/ocean territories |
| | Church of Steam | Significant land influence *before* the God War |
| | Church of the Earth | Missionary rights only pre-God War; extraordinary teams after |
| **Intis Republic** | Church of the Sun | |
| | Church of Steam | Roselle's influence; open, steam-punk character |
| **Fenepot** | Church of the Earth | |
| | Church of Knowledge | Early 5th Epoch only (now independent in Lunburg) |
| **Lunburg** | Church of Knowledge | Small country; once protected by Intis |
| **Feysac Empire** | Church of God of War | Pre-God War timeline |

*Allied churches share the same region. Churches of adjacent pathways are never allies (they represent potential rivals).*

### Official Beyonder Benefits

**Stable income:** A clerk earns ~5 pounds/week; field agents and investigators earn ~8 pounds/week. Travel expenses on official missions are reimbursable; death-in-service grants family a pension equal to one year's salary.

**Reputation boost:** Reputation skill rises by one level.

**A Beyonder path:** Choose a path your church controls and become a Beyonder. You receive the Sequence 9 formula and must submit it for verification before using it. All sources of potions, characteristics, or formulas must be reported.

**Skill training:** The church helps you hire teachers. Gain two skill improvements of one level each (not Reputation).

**Extraordinary bullets** (2d3 per mission): Choose type per bullet:
- *Purifying Bullets:* +1d6 damage against undead.
- *Hunting Bullets:* Effective against Beyonders; treat as if you have one Sequence level higher for purposes of overcoming their resistance.
- *Tracing Bullets:* If the bullet hits, you can track the target for 24 hours via divination.
- *Freezing Bullets:* Hit imposes Cold damage and a −2 Movement penalty for 1 round.

**Sealed Item access:** After accumulating enough merit, you may request temporary use of church-held Sealed Items. Discuss specifics with your GM.

**Restrictions:** Your actions are observed by the church. Unauthorized promotions are very difficult to justify. Leaving is complicated. Demigod promotion involves very limited organizational support.

## Expansion 3: Special Races

> This expansion provides **setting flavor and optional mechanical bonuses**. If the GM disables the stat bonuses, players may still choose these racial backgrounds — they simply won't receive the listed numerical benefits.
>
> Never ban a player from a racial background solely because of Expansion 3's existence.

### 1. Blood Race (Vampires)

The Blood Race descends from Lilith, who fell in the Second Era. They are one of the most powerful factions remaining, with several angels among their members and a vast collection of Sealed Items. Unlike the vampires of legend, the Blood Race *respects life* (Lilith's influence) — they are not hostile to the Orthodox Churches, though an uneasy tolerance is the norm.

**Rogue vampires** who kill freely are either traitors who joined the Rose School or **artificial vampires** (ordinary humans turned by a Moon-path Beyonder).

**To become a natural vampire (if not born one):** Find a true vampire, earn their approval, and have them perform the *First Embrace* ritual (requires their excess Sequence 7 characteristic). You join their family, take their surname, and potentially a new name.

**Natural Vampire Stats:**
- Start as a natural **Sequence 7 Moon-path** Beyonder.
- Minimum Charisma: 3 (so +1 if your base was 2).
- Physical appearance is fixed in adulthood (~70–100 years of age subjectively; looks like a young adult).
- Pointed ears, striking eyes (customizable shades of red), and notable beauty.
- Cannot join an Orthodox Church team officially.

**Advancement challenges:** Moon-path characteristics are monopolized by the Blood Race. Main ingredients for Sequence 7+ are effectively the characteristics of other vampires. Promotion queues within the clan can stretch thousands of years without notable merit. Finding an outside Sequence 6 characteristic bypasses the queue.

**Social obligations:** Aristocratic education and etiquette are expected. You belong to a noble family within the clan.

**Knowledge growth:** Every 50 years of active learning grants Education +1 and one skill level increase (up to 3 stacks via gameplay).

**Enemies:** Rose School and artificial vampires (who subsist by killing natural vampires for their characteristics).

---

### 2. Feysac (Giants' Descendants)

The Feysac Empire's people are naturally tall and powerful — averaging over 2 meters — due to generations of God-of-War-path influence in the local environment.

**Feysac Benefits:**
- Strength and Physique each start at minimum 3.
- Size class: Large (6).
- Naturally intimidating; Intimidate skill starts at Trained.

---

### 3. Elves and Giant Descendants

Rare bloodlines that survive into the Fifth Epoch. Consult your GM for specific benefits — they vary greatly by lineage and the degree of dilution over generations.

## Expansion 4: Economy and Lifestyle

### Wealth and Daily Life

Reputation represents your accessible personal funds (see Chapter 4). Beyond that, day-to-day expenses include:

- **Room and board:** Roughly 1–2 shillings/day for modest lodgings and meals.
- **Private investigation fees:** A typical Beyonder investigator charges 5–15 pounds per case.
- **Potion ingredients:** Extremely variable; the main ingredient for a Sequence 9 potion may cost anywhere from a few pounds to over a hundred depending on rarity.

### Working an Honest Job

If downtime is available, characters can earn income:
- Skilled labor (trained artisan, tutor): ~1–3 pounds/week
- White-collar work (accountant, junior clerk): ~2–5 pounds/week
- Investigator-for-hire: ~5–15 pounds per case
- Spending 1 extra hour every morning for work: Luck check +2 (favorable).
- Spending 3 extra hours per morning: Luck check +2, plus one additional effect of your choice (with GM approval).

## Expansion 5: Beyonders in Special States

Not all Beyonders follow the normal 22-pathway advancement. The following special states may arise in play, or be available to players with GM permission.

### 1. Failed Promotion

A Beyonder who narrowly survived an out-of-control promotion and did not successfully advance. They emerged incomplete.

**Consequences:**
- If the failed characteristics are still in their body: They function as a half-powered version of the next Sequence (all abilities at half effect, rounded up). Cannot attempt promotion again until characteristics are extracted. Personality suppression also halved. Witnessing incomplete mythological forms deals halved Sanity loss.
- **Terminal illness:** Within 5 years, a lethal disease sets in. Life expectancy: ~1 more year. Successful promotion during this window restores normal lifespan.
- The illness can be suppressed (Moon/Earth path Sequence 4+ may help), but not fully cured without advancement.

### 2. Born Incomplete Beyonder

Born with half a Sequence 9 characteristic already inside them.

**Status:**
- Receive half the attribute/skill bonuses of their Sequence 9 potion (round down; roll 1d2 for single-bonus potions).
- Can only take the corresponding Sequence 9 potion. Taking Sequence 8 is dangerous.
- Digestibility cap: 10 (instead of 20). All Beyonder abilities at half effect (rounded up).

### 3. Descendants of the Abraham Family

*(Limited to timelines when Mr. Door is sealed)*

Every full moon and blood moon, these characters hear anguished cries from their ancestor, Mr. Door. Their family calls it a blood curse.

**Effects:**
- **Full moon:** 2+1d3 minutes of uncontrollable semi-out-of-control state. Losing 2 Sanity during this time causes immediate loss of control.
- **Blood moon:** 7+1d3 minutes instead.
- Every promotion attempt automatically fails the Sanity check unless a shelter is in place.
- Paths other than the Apprenticeship path carry additional risk.
- The Abraham family provides sufficient resources for the Apprenticeship path (formula, main materials, auxiliary materials).
- If the character chooses not to become a Beyonder, none of the above effects apply.

### 4. Demigod Evil Spirit (Post-Death)

When a Sequence 4 Beyonder dies, they have 5 minutes of activity with their remaining spirit — potentially longer if strong emotions remain. A Beyonder with unresolved attachments may choose to transform into an **evil spirit** rather than pass on.

**Evil Spirit Abilities:**
- Gains: Wraith Scream, Wraith Possession, Mirror Image Jump, Spirit World Shuttle.
- Classified as undead: 5-point resistance to curse, cold, and poison; attacks deal 3d6 cold damage.
- Without their characteristics: Can only maintain existence within 100 meters of a fixed location. Leaving for 72+ hours causes dissolution in 24 hours.
- Can drain spirituality from nearby creatures (1 spirituality = 1 minute of extra existence time). Sustained draining drives targets toward madness.
- No longer needs potions or ritual ceremonies to accommodate Beyonder characteristics — but must still follow path-jump rules.
- As a Full-Round action, may devour the soul of a helpless living creature: recover 1d6 + their remaining Spirituality (max: double your Spirituality cap).

### 5. Forced Path Jump (Brave Jump)

A Beyonder who forcibly took a non-adjacent or incompatible jump, gaining the combined powers of two pathways — at the cost of their sanity and any hope of further advancement.

**Status:**
- Permanently in **Uncertain Madness** (cannot be cured, only suppressed).
- Will Defense and Sanity limit permanently halved.
- Will checks permanently −2 (unfavorable).
- Gains all Beyonder abilities from both accommodated pathway characteristics (customize the hybrid effects with your GM).
- Whenever combat begins, when suffering mental damage, or upon any Sanity loss: immediately roll a madness tendency and act on it.

### 6. Aged Beyonder / Aged Ordinary Person

Before Sequence 4, Beyonders eventually age. After Sequence 4–3, aging simply comes much later.

**Age thresholds (ordinary person equivalent):**
- Mid-to-low Sequence: "Old" at 70; "Very old" at 30 years from max lifespan.
- Sequence 4–3: "Old" at 270; "Very old" at 100 years before max lifespan.

**Aging Penalties:**
- Agility, Physique, and Strength permanently −1. If within half of remaining lifespan: −2.
- Failed Beyonder ability checks immediately cost 0/1 Sanity and HP equal to Spirituality consumed.
- Promotion Sanity check: +5 (unfavorable). Every additional year of age adds +1 more. A 100-year-old elder at the low-sequence cap might face a +35 penalty. Semi-out-of-control during promotion = instant loss of control.

## Expansion 6: Beyonder Animals

Beyonder animals are a unique feature of the Lord of Mysteries world — potion ingredients, companion creatures, and occasionally powerful entities in their own right.

### Ordinary Animals (cats, dogs, rats, etc.)

Starting as a small or medium animal:
- Education minimum: 1.
- Inspiration +2 (sharper instincts than humans).
- Stealth increases by one level (people don't pay attention to animals).
- Agility and one other appropriate attribute +1.
- Cannot meditate — promotion check +4 (unfavorable) without an animal trainer partner.
- Once promoted (if the potion improves Intelligence), may learn to speak a human language.

### Born Supernatural Animals (Potion Ingredients)

- Begin with roughly half the abilities of their pathway (Sequence 3 and below; excludes vampires).
- Typically hostile to humans on sight; must be handled carefully.
- Appearance and nature are player/GM-defined.

### Uncommon Animals (lions, tigers, baboons)

- Medium to large size class.
- Performance checks: +4 (you attract attention wherever you go).
- Stealth: −4 (impossible to ignore).
- Once promoted (Intelligence-boosting potion), may learn to speak human languages.

## Expansion 7: Extraordinary Derivatives

The original work leaves many blank spaces in the 22 pathways — the author explicitly notes that descriptions of dark magic include "but not limited to" language, and that qualified potion teachers develop their own variants.

This expansion allows GMs and players to create:
- **New potion formulas** for existing sequences (variant potions that provide alternate abilities within the same pathway theme)
- **Hybrid abilities** developed through deep study, unique ingredients, or significant in-world experimentation
- **Custom sealed items** crafted from particularly significant Beyonder characteristic combinations

**Guidelines:**
- New formulas must stay thematically consistent with their sequence level and pathway.
- Variant potions occupy the same "slot" in the pathway progression — they don't grant additional sequence levels.
- All new creations require GM review and approval before use in play.
- The intensity of a derivative formula should not exceed that of a standard same-sequence potion.
