## What Is Ritual Magic?

Ritual magic is the practice of arranging a ceremonial space over time, then praying to a higher power for assistance, spells, or information. For low-sequence Beyonders, it is the primary means of accessing divine power beyond their own abilities. For high-sequence Beyonders (Sequence 3+), they can begin praying to themselves, and may create even more powerful artifacts from high-level spiritual materials.

---

## General Ritual Process

### Step 1 — Prepare the Space

Find a quiet, undisturbed location. If outdoors, sprinkle **Amanda Hydrosol** to create a tranquil atmosphere and meet the minimum requirement.

### Step 2 — Choose Your Deity

You may only safely pray to **the Seven Righteous Gods** by default. Praying to hidden existences risks attracting dangerous attention.

### Step 3 — Gather Materials

**Core materials (always required):**
- Three candles: two for the deity (upper left and upper right of the altar), one for yourself (center)
- A vessel: small silver bowl, cauldron, or similar
- A **Spiritual Wall** tool: silver knife **or** holy night powder
- Hydrosols, essential oils, and herbs symbolic of your chosen deity
- Parchment, ink, a quill (or functional equivalents)
- Coarse salt and clean water (for the silver knife method)

**For spell/talisman/enchanted bullet making only:**
- The specific carrier material for your path's spell (see Spell Materials table below)
- Surface must be engraved with the corresponding deity's symbols

**For abilities requiring ritual enhancement only (if the caster hasn't gained ritual power from a potion):**
- Supplementary items to guide spirituality: bells, crystal balls, silver cups, incense

### Step 4 — Build the Spiritual Wall

The Spiritual Wall is an invisible barrier that shields the ritual area from external influence. Any physical disturbance (opening a door, being attacked, etc.) breaks it.

| Method | Action Cost | Spirituality | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Holy Night Powder** | 1 Casting action | 1 per 5m / 2 per 10m | One-time material; creates a sealed area. Briefly refreshing: +1 to Inspiration checks for 1 round. |
| **Silver Knife** | 1 Full-round action per 5m | 1 per 5m | Must first sanctify the knife in coarse salt with a Hermetic incantation, then insert into clean water. Then trace the spiritual wall with the knife tip. |
| **Spiritual Creature** | 1 Casting or Full-round | 1 per 5m | No knife or powder needed; fingertips emit spirituality directly. |

> Non-ritual-enhanced Beyonders (those without a potion that grants ritual qualification) need an additional Casting action to build the wall, using supplementary items to guide their spirituality.

**Sample Knife Sanctification Incantation:**
> *"I sanctify you, Silver Blade! I cleanse and purify you so that you can serve me in rituals! In the name of the Goddess of the Night, Lord of Scarlet, you are sanctified!"*

### Step 5 — Open the Ritual

1. Use 1 Casting action to enter **meditation**, then light the three candles left to right.
2. Build the Spiritual Wall (may be done in advance).
3. At this point, environmental effects tied to the deity may manifest — e.g., the Goddess of the Night causes nearby streetlights to extinguish.

### Step 6 — Invoke and Pray

1. Drip the corresponding essential oil onto the candles; burn the corresponding herbs in the vessel. A scented mist fills the area.
2. On the parchment, draw or write the symbols corresponding to the power you seek. (To dispel a debt: draw a framed box around it, then a cross through it. To avoid disaster: draw avoidance symbols.)
3. **Chant the deity's honored name** — this is required to direct the prayer. Hidden existences don't have automatic responses, but can still be named (with risk).
4. Speak your prayer clearly and respectfully. No specific script is required — sincerity matters more than phrasing.
5. Name the herbs and pleasing materials you've prepared and thank the deity for receiving them. *(This step can be skipped if the deity must respond anyway.)*

### Step 7 (Interrupted Ritual only) — Resume if Needed

If you left the ritual area mid-prayer (see Interrupted Ritual below), return to the altar, refill the Spiritual Wall exit (a Swift action), and use **1 Casting action + 2 Swift actions** to re-enter meditation and continue the prayer.

### Step 8 — Complete the Ritual

Add a drop of essential oil to each candle, then burn the parchment in the vessel. The ritual is complete.

---

**Sample Prayer** *(from the original work, Vol. 1, Ch. 86)*:
> *"Goddess of the Night, loftier than the stars and older than eternity;*
> *I pray for your favor.*
> *I pray that you will take care of one of your faithful believers.*
> *I pray for the power of Scarlet; I pray for the power of adversity and fear.*
> *I pray that you will let your faithful believer, Selena Wood, be freed from the taint of evil and from the entanglement of misfortune.*
> *I beg you — wait a moment, wait for that unfortunate girl."*

> All ritual speech should be in a **Mystical Language** (Hermetic, Ancient Hermetic, etc.). Hermetic must be *spoken aloud* to function; other mystical languages can be read silently. Spoken Hermetic is safer — silent chanting of honored names while half-asleep can accidentally address a deity.

## Ritual Check Summary

**Standard ritual:** ~5 minutes of effort; roll **Difficulty 20 Occultism** (can be replaced by roleplaying the full ritual process).

**Additional checks:**
- Praying to a deity you don't believe in: Also roll **Difficulty 25 Luck**. Critical failure distorts the effect.
- Being an ordinary person, or praying to a hidden existence: Always requires a Luck check.
- Correct pleasing materials at the ceremony: **−2 (favorable)** to Luck check.
- Ritual involves demigod-level power: Occultism must be at **Erudite** level minimum.
- Superficial belief (not a core church member, patron donor, or noble): Luck check required even for your own deity.
- Praying without performing ritual magic (just chanting the name): Requires being at least Sequence 5, a bishop/senior deacon, or a personal favorite. Otherwise, Difficulty 20 Luck check required.
- Praying to a deity hostile to your own: Severe risk — punishment or obliteration on a failed check.

## Holy Days and Timing

Each of the Seven Righteous Gods has a corresponding day and hour. Performing ritual magic at the right time significantly improves success.

| Day | Deity | Planet | Domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Mother Earth (Earth) | Saturn (brown) | Change, acceptance, pure femininity |
| **Tuesday** | Lord of Storms (Storm) | Jupiter (orange) | Expansion, luck, positive energy |
| **Wednesday** | God of War | Mars (red) | Conflict, momentum, positive bias |
| **Thursday** | Eternal Blazing Sun (Sun) | Sun | Energy, creation, pure masculinity |
| **Friday** | God of Steam and Machinery (Steam) | Venus | Harmony, balance, femininity |
| **Saturday** | God of Knowledge and Wisdom (Reader) | Mercury (blue) | Reason, communication, neutral |
| **Sunday** | Goddess of the Night (Evernight) | Moon | Restriction, contraction, femininity |

**Known optimal hours:**
- *Moon Hour (Evernight):* 2–3am, 9–10am, 4–5pm, 11pm–midnight (Sunday)
- *Blue Star Hour (Knowledge):* 12am–1am Friday, 11am–noon Saturday, 4–5pm (unspecified day), 11pm–midnight (unspecified day)

**Effect of correct timing:** Ordinary people praying to their own deity no longer need a Luck check. Occultism difficulty and Luck difficulty for non-believing prayers: **−5** each. Correct day and correct hour stack (−10 total).

## Spell-Making Ritual

Before making a spell, you need a **carrier material** — a physical medium engraved with the deity's symbols.

**Carrier Materials by Path:**

| Path | Material | Approximate Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Fool | Pure silver + quicksilver | Moderate |
| Apprentice (Gate) | Gems | Expensive |
| Perfector | Bronze | Moderate |
| Sun | Gold | Expensive |
| Storm | Tin | Cheap (measured in soli) |
| Reader | Quicksilver + brass | Moderate |
| God of War | Iron | Low |
| Evernight (Night) | Sterling silver | Moderate |
| Moon | Silver or sterling silver | Moderate |
| Earth | Lead | Low |

*(Unlisted paths: check via a Difficulty 15 Credit/Reputation check to source materials.)*

**Crafting the carrier:** Difficulty 15 Craft Manufacturing check to carve the material into a palm-sized charm shape and engrave symbols on both sides.

**During the ritual:** Place the prepared carrier on the altar. In your prayer, clearly state the spell ability you want imprinted on it (choose from Sequence 9–6 of the deity's path). Sequence 5 spells require Erudite-level Occultism.

**Special — God of Knowledge and Wisdom:** Spells made through the God of Knowledge may draw from *any* path (Sequence 9–6), but the resulting power is halved in effect. (Reader-path casters are exempt from this halving.)

**Shelf life:** Spells last **one week** before the spirituality dissipates. If stored over 4 days, power is halved. You may recharge it with another short ritual before expiration.

**Demigod-level spells** require a demigod's own blood as an additional component.

## Interrupted Ritual Magic

For grand rituals that take hours (or even a full day), the **Interrupted Ritual** technique allows you to briefly leave the ritual area while keeping the prayer active.

**Requirements:**
- The deity must have already responded before you leave.
- You must explicitly ask the deity to wait in your prayer: *"I beg you — wait a moment…"*

**Caution:** Do not use casual language when asking a deity to wait. Irreverent phrasing ("hold on, I need to use the bathroom") has reportedly had fatal results.

**Process:**
1. After successfully establishing contact, cut an "exit" in the Spiritual Wall using the silver knife.
2. Leave freely, carry out your other tasks, bring a target into the ritual space, etc.
3. Return, refill the exit (Swift action), resume at the altar with **1 Casting action + 2 Swift actions**.

**Tactical use:** This allows you to lure enemies into a pre-prepared ritual area, then seal it behind them and complete the ritual to call down divine power.

## Invocation of Power Ritual

A simplified ritual that skips spell-making — instead of creating a charm, you pray for the deity to directly apply their power *within the ritual area*.

- Process is identical to the standard ritual.
- Change your prayer from "make me a spell" to "please act on my behalf against this target."
- **Warning:** You pay all the Spirituality consumed by the deity. Invoking too powerful an effect may drain you completely.

## Divination Ritual (Magic Mirror)

An advanced form of ritual used to ask the gods for information rather than power.

**Requirements:** A silver-plated mirror; the standard ritual process; relevant clues or divination sentences prepared in advance.

Because the deity responds, the ritual succeeds by default — but you still need meaningful clues to give them something to work with.

**Ordinary people:** Must perform the ritual on the appropriate holy day. Must bathe. Pray seven times, then drop the mirror very gently — the broken pattern is the answer. Interpreting it requires Proficient Occultism or a Difficulty 25 check.

**Beyonders:** See images or text directly in the mirror. Information specificity is GM-determined.

**Warning:** The mirror divination lacks the protection of a spiritual wall. The responding deity can emerge through the mirror, possess you, or leave supernatural sequelae. Praying to hidden existences for mirror divination is particularly dangerous. Only pray to the Seven Righteous Gods unless you have strong reasons otherwise.

## Necromantic Ritual (Channeling the Dead)

On the standard ritual foundation, with at least some spiritual presence in the area.

**Contacting a fresh spirit:** If a body has died within the last day, its soul hasn't fully dissipated. Channel through the ritual.

**For non-Death-path Beyonders:** Use **dream divination** during the ritual — write a divination sentence, then use it to observe the deceased's memories. This yields **1d2 answers**.

**Limits:**
- Spirits over one week old fully dissipate — unchannelable.
- Spirits more than one day old become invisible to non-Death-path Beyonders; location can only be approximated.
- The older the death, the vaguer the information.

**Channeling the Living (Psychic):** The target must be asleep, unconscious, or willing (consenting targets can use Amanda Hydrosol + Spirit Eye Drops for a gentler process). Unlike channeling the dead, the living communicant speaks normally and answers questions without limit.

**Warning:** Channeling a living person who is insane forces you to make the same Sanity check that caused their madness. Taking a *Tranquility Potion* protects against this — unless the madness involves corruption 1 Sequence level above you.

## Seance Ritual (Directed Communication)

A variant of the necromantic ritual that establishes direct two-way dialogue with the subject being prayed for (rather than just extracting memories).

**Vessel:** Prepare a paper figurine or similar object to serve as a physical anchor for the responding spirit. Without one, the responding entity may instead possess the ritual host — either fully (host loses consciousness) or partially (host remains aware while the entity speaks).

**Note:** High-sequence beings (angels and above) may not respond in the expected way — they are under no obligation to follow the ritual's rules of engagement.

---

## Portrait Ritual Magic

A specialized technique for creating a spiritually resonant portrait of a target.

**Process:** Choose a target you have information about (same requirements as divination — you need clues or belongings). Perform the general ritual. Upon completion, your body automatically picks up a brush and paints a lifelike portrait of the target at tremendous speed.

**Anti-divination:** The portrait ritual is subject to anti-divination effects. However, because it acts on the *scene* rather than you personally, the target's own anti-divination only applies if it can counter mystical information in the environment — not just self-directed anti-divination.

**Memory portraits:** If you're painting from your own memory (someone you've seen before), this bypasses anti-divination entirely. However, if the target was disguised when you saw them, the portrait depicts the disguise.

---

## Solidification Ritual Magic

Advanced ritual magic that creates **permanent** (or long-lasting) magical effects rather than temporary ones.

**Requirements:** At minimum, a meaningful personal component must be included — often something irreplaceable. The strength and longevity of the solidified effect scales with the value of what is sacrificed and the quality of the ritual.

**Typical uses:** Permanent protective wards, long-term blessings, binding seals, and territorial effects.

**Warning:** Solidification ritual magic is beyond what is achievable with simple Sequence 9–8 Occultism. It generally requires at least Erudite-level Occultism and a meaningful offering.
